How it works

Type a place. Watch the world walk into it.

The tapestry never stops scrolling. Anyone watching can say where it goes next, and a few seconds later the painting turns toward it, seamlessly.

  1. 01  Type an idea

    A place, a mood, something ridiculous. No account needed.

  2. 02  It joins the queue

    Sort by Up next, New or Most liked, and heart the ones you want to see. When it's busy, everyone takes turns.

  3. 03  Watch it land

    Your idea scrolls in for everyone watching, titled, with its own page you can share.
