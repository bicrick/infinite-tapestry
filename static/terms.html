Terms & content policy

Infinite Tapestry is one endless painting that everyone steers together. A few simple rules keep it a place worth watching.

What you can suggest

Places, moods, styles, stories, strange ideas. Short is fine. Auto, the tapestry's director, interprets every idea and may reshape, merge or skip it.

What it will never paint

  • Sexual or nude content of any kind.
  • Anything sexualising, endangering or depicting harm to minors. Zero tolerance.
  • Graphic violence, gore, or self-harm.
  • Hate, harassment, or targeting real private people.
  • Spam, ads, or attempts to flood the queue.

How ideas are handled

Anyone can add ideas without an account; signing in lets you add more, vote and keep a profile. Every idea is checked automatically before it's considered. Ideas that break these rules are dropped, and repeated attempts can quietly stop an account, browser or network from contributing. We may also remove or ban at our discretion.

Your submissions

By submitting, you confirm the idea (and any image) is yours to share, and you grant Infinite Tapestry a worldwide, royalty-free licence to use it to create, display and share the tapestry. Painted scenes become part of the public canvas and can be viewed and shared by anyone.

Privacy, briefly

For guests we keep a random handle, a signed cookie, and salted hashes of your network address and a few coarse browser traits, only to apply limits and stop abuse. We don't sell data or run ad trackers.

No guarantees

The tapestry is an experiment provided as is. It may pause, change, or lose content. Payments (fuel) only fund painting time and aren't refundable once used.

Questions or a report? Reach the maintainer through the site's X account.
