Infinite Tapestry: a live AI art stream and endless AI painting

Infinite Tapestry is an endless AI painting that scrolls past live, 24 hours a day. It never repeats and never ends: each new stretch of the scroll is painted in real time, and anyone watching can type an idea to steer where it goes next. It is infinite AI art you can steer, together with everyone else watching.

Leave it running fullscreen as a free AI screensaver or ambient art for a TV or second monitor, replay any moment in the Time Machine, or read how Infinite Tapestry works.
